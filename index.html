The three goals of every call — nothing more
  • 1. Confirm it landed. Make sure the proposal we emailed on October 20 or 21 actually reached them and was seen.
  • 2. Reach the decision-maker. Get the name of the owner, manager or events lead — and their direct email. If the first person you reach isn't the decision-maker, leaving with that name and email is a successful call.
  • 3. Close one concrete step. A 10-minute live demo this week, or a conversation about the 7-day free trial — card required upfront, charged only from day 8 if you continue. Never a vague "we'll think about it."

When to call: Tuesday to Thursday, 2:00–4:00 PM — between the lunch and dinner rush for restaurants, a quieter hour for clinics. Maximum two attempts per business; on the second, leave the voicemail script and stop. Log the outcome on the card below the moment you hang up — that's the trail the whole team works from.
