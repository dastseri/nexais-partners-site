Commissions

What you earn — and how it stacks
How it works

On monthly AVA contracts you earn 30% of the first month, then 10% of every month for the life of the contract. On one-time projects — websites, films, design work — you earn 20% of the project value, paid when the client has paid in full. Commissions are calculated on amounts actually received from the client and are paid monthly.
